FAQ

Quick answers before exploring the data

What data does this website use?

The site uses vaccination and infection data from World Health Organization sources.

Who is this website for?

It is designed for parents, general users, and health officers who need clear vaccination information.

Can users compare countries or years?

Yes. Users can filter by infection type, year, and economic group to compare results across countries.

Why use rates per 100,000 people?

This makes countries easier to compare even when their population sizes are different.
